Draw& Guess

⚠️ This isn't a guild name, so you can play as a guest but your points won't be saved to the Hall of Fame.

How to play

  1. When it's your turn, choose a word and draw it.
  2. Everyone else types their guesses in the chat.
  3. The faster you guess, the more points you get. The drawer gets points too!

    Werewolf

    ⚠️ This isn't a guild name, so you can play as a guest but your wins won't be saved to the Hall of Fame.

    How to play

    Werewolves are hiding in the village. Every night they secretly eat someone. Every day the village talks and votes to lynch whoever they think is a wolf. Villagers win when every wolf is gone; wolves win when they match the villagers. 5 to 20 players.
