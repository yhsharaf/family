      BonkDuel

      ⚠️ This isn't a guild name, so you can duel as a guest but your wins won't be saved to the Hall of Fame.
      Pick your class
      🍄 Practice against a monster

      How to play

      You both pick a move in secret, then both are revealed at once. Read your opponent! First to knock out the other's 20 HP wins. Moves cost MP (you get +1 every turn, max 5). From turn 10 it's sudden death: every hit does +1. Your class gives you a 6th move and a passive.

      🎯 Critical tap: when your hit lands, a ring shrinks over the enemy. Tap the arena when it lines up for +1 damage!

      👥 2v2: same moves, but every attack picks which enemy to hit, and Shield can cover you or your teammate. Both teammates hitting the same enemy is a COMBO (+2). Once per team, a Revive (3 MP) brings a knocked-out teammate back with 5 HP. Plan with the secret team signals 🤫

          StarForce

          ⚠️ This isn't a guild name: you can enhance as a guest, but you won't show on the guild leaderboard.
          🃏 Boom Roulette · the multiplayer liar's game at a Kerning City table Play ▶

          📈 Tries to reach each star

          🏆 Guild leaderboard

          🏅 Score = how hard your best star is × how lucky you got (the average cost of that star ÷ what you spent, square-rooted). Spending keeps counting after your best, so knowing when to stop and 📤 share is the strategy. Only shared results count.

            Rates, costs and stats are MapleStory Idle's (from mapleidle.gg). From 20★ an item can be destroyed 💥: it falls back to 12★ with its effects off, and you restore it at 12★–20★ (higher costs more), just like the game. Two modes: 🔥 Start at 20★ (every item begins at 20★, straight into the scary part) or 🌱 Start at 0★; each has its own boards. Switching modes throws your results away unless you share them first. You enhance a set of 8 items (switch with the item buttons). Nothing counts until you press 📤 Share results: then your set is scored, goes on the board, and all 8 items start again from 0★ (🆕 New game also starts from 0★, without sharing). The score rewards high stars, but also luck and knowing when to stop: it compares what each item really cost with what that star costs on average. The Fastest 30★ board is one item, counted the moment it hits 30★. From 12★ each star has its own Enhancement Points gauge (fail +1, drop +2, boom +4, kept on that star even if you drop); when it's full the next try at that star can't fail. Mitigation (as in the game's Sept 3 patch notes, only from 20★ where the risks start): destruction mitigation halves the destroy chance, decrease mitigation removes the drop chance (halves it at 28★+); each costs one extra full set of scrolls and mesos (both = ×3). Scrolls here are unlimited, every try is rolled by the guild server so the board is fair.
